Biograph:

Wenpeng Lu is a Professor and PhD Supervisor at Qilu University of Technology (Shandong Academy of Sciences), and a recipient of the Shandong Provincial Outstanding Graduate Supervisor award. He obtained his PhD in Computer Application Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2014. From October 2016 to October 2017, Wenpeng was a visiting researcher at the Advanced Analytics Institute (AAi), University of Technology Sydney.

Wenpeng serves as the Director of the Natural Language Processing and Cognitive Computing Team at the university, and the Director of the Jinan Municipal Engineering Research Center for Medical and Health Information Processing. He is a member of the Natural Language Processing Technical Committee of the China Computer Federation (CCF), and a member of the Youth Working Committee of the Chinese Information Processing Society of China (CIPS), as well as a committee member of the CIPS Technical Committees on Sentiment Computing, Machine Translation and Multilingual Information Processing, and Foundation Models and Generation. He also serves as a Vice Chair of the AI + Healthcare Management Branch of the Shandong Medical Doctor Association, and as a council member of the Shandong Artificial Intelligence Society. Wenpeng has actively organized academic events, serving as Local Chair for the 24th China Conference on Computational Linguistics (CCL 2025) and the 19th China Conference on Machine Translation (CCMT 2023), and organizing the CIPS Youth Working Committee academic salon at Qilu University of Technology, the Frontier Forum on Natural Language Processing, and a frontier technology tutorial on “Training Methods and Industrial Applications of Large Models.” He has also been invited to serve as an Area Chair (AC) for the ACL track of CCF A-category conferences, and as an Editorial Board Member for Springer’s SCI journal International Journal of Data Science and Analytics (JDSA) and the EI-indexed journal Discover Artificial Intelligence.


Research Interest:

Large Language Models and Agents, Natural Language Processing, Medical and Health Information Processing, Recommendation Systems
